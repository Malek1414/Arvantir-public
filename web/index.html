How it works

Five steps, from a niche to a ranked list.

You type a niche. Arvantir finds public lists of it, reads every company on them, checks which ones a big database already has, and ranks the rest by fit.

  1. 1 Find listsTrade directories, member lists, registries for the niche.
  2. 2 Read companiesOne clean row per company, duplicates merged.
  3. 3 Check databaseIs it in a 17 million company database? If not, lead databases most likely miss it too.
  4. 4 EnrichDecision maker from the company's legal notice, news and hiring from the last 6 months.
  5. 5 RankFit score, tier A to D and a legal channel. Every company links to where it was found.

What changes for Arvana.

Arvana keeps Clay and its workflow. Arvantir sits in front: it finds the companies databases miss, mostly local businesses, and enriches the ones they already know.
